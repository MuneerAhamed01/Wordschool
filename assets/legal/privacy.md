# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how WordSchool (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information in the WordSchool app.

## What we collect

- Account information: If you sign in, we receive your user ID and, when available, your display name and email via Firebase Authentication.
- Gameplay data: Word attempts, progress, and basic app settings to provide core gameplay features and persistence.
- Device information: Non‑identifying information like OS version and app version for support and quality.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, and we do not use analytics or ad networks.

## How we use information

- Provide, personalize, and improve gameplay features
- Authenticate users and sync progress across sessions/devices
- Support, safety, and debugging (e.g., investigating issues)

## Children’s privacy (mixed audience)

WordSchool is suitable for a general audience and may be used by children. We collect only the minimum information needed to provide the service. We do not show targeted ads. Parents or guardians can request review or deletion of a child’s account data by contacting us (see Contact).

## Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where applicable, we rely on:
- Performance of a contract (to provide the app and its features)
- Legitimate interests (to maintain and secure the service)
- Consent where required by law

## Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the app (e.g., Firebase) under appropriate safeguards.

## Data retention

We retain data only as long as needed for gameplay, legal, or operational purposes. You may request deletion at any time (see Contact).

## Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted in‑app.
