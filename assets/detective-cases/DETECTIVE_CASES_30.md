**Difficulty:** Medium

### Clue 1

**Answer:** RIFLE

### Clue 2

### Clue 3

### Final Hint

### Word Meaning

**Rifle**

Pronunciation: /ˈraɪfl/  
Noun

A gun with a long barrel, fired from the shoulder.

### Vocabulary

**Synonyms:** Gun, Firearm, Weapon  
**Antonyms:** Shield, Peace

### Example Sentence

"Powder residue on the hearth confirmed the cabin rifle was fired indoors."
